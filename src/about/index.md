### About Christ the Reconciler

%%rewrite in reference to changes on the homepage—in elgin, near austin, residential and non-residential, etc.%%

We are a missional community of prayer and reconciliation in Elgin, Texas. We are on sixteen acres of land, a shared household, and a growing network of Christians who believe that the divisions fracturing the Body of Christ are not inevitable.

We are not trying to dissolve our differences. We are trying to end the hostility.

### Are We Christian? Are We Safe?

We get it. An ecumenical intentional community can raise eyebrows. Any community is a risk. So let's be direct.

%%this addresses the control issue%%
Christ the Reconciler is rooted in historic Christianity. Christianity has, historically, recommended community. Every member of our community is an active, committed member of their own church, parish, or congregation — Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox, or Messianic Jewish. We do not ask anyone to leave their tradition. We do not create a new denomination. We are not a replacement for your local church. We affirm the ancient faith shared across the Christian world. Our community members hold real theological, political, sports teams, etc. differences.

How do we protect kids? How do I leave? Are you taking my money?

We have been commended by Catholic priests and Protestant pastors, by Pentecostals and Anabaptists, by leaders in the US and across Europe.

### What We Believe

We don't hold a lowest-common-denominator faith. We hold _our_ faiths — Catholic, Protestant, Orthodox, Messianic — and we hold them seriously, with conviction and love.

What brings us together is not agreement on every doctrine. It is a shared conviction that Jesus meant what he prayed, and that Christian hostility is a sin worth repenting of. We believe reconciliation is not a feeling or a metaphor — it is a concrete set of actions that removes hostility, repairs damage, and restores relationship.

The ancient faith of the church — the Trinitarian God, the incarnation, death and resurrection of Jesus, the work of the Holy Spirit — is the ground we stand on together. Beyond that, we hold our differences with honesty and mutual respect, not pretending they don't exist, and not letting them become weapons.

### How People Relate to CTR

There are many ways to be part of what we're doing.

%%add links to each%%

**Friends, supporters, and distant participants** — People who follow along, pray with us, listen to our teachings, and support the work financially.

**Way of Life** — available without official anything for anyone anywhere

**Rule of Life** — This is membership. Each year, individuals around the world have the opportunity to make a personal commitment to living out John 17 in their own life. This is following the rule of life and adopting the Statement. [[ctr-practices-john17|Learn more]]

**Common Life Commitment** — Requires proximity. A deeper level of commitment to living out John 17 together in the geographic proximity of Austin, Texas as a community.

**Residential Commitment** — When housing is complete, there will be a Residential Rule for those who are called to live here full-time. This is one expression of commitment and not the goal for everyone.

Our goal is not that everyone becomes a resident. Our goal is that each person engages at the level they are called to.
